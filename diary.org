* october 2026

Since July, I've been working on a device whose aim it is to provide one
essential feature of our smartphones (async communication), without all of the
distraction. I have a working prototype, yay! And using it, [I felt how it
helped me retain uninterrupted flow of thoughts].

It makes me very motivated to keep working on this. It's sad to think how rare
it has become to experience a mind that's not craving random pieces of external
information all the time. Our continuous desire for a device to tell us what to
think about next is strange and exhausting!

* june 2026

We've grown tired of smartphones. But what could be a new direction for
personal tech? [I wrote about what using an iPod taught me]: our interactions
should be (designs) single-purpose and intentional.

In the same vein, I created a communication app called [pager] that allows you
to send one message to someone's menu bar. I've been using it for a few months
with a person I'm seeing, and it really gives you this feeling of putting a
note in the lunch-box of a loved one.

Spending time in Barcelona the past few months made me realize there is just no
good cultural event calendar, so I built one at [cartelita.co]! It's strongly
inspired by the great [jazzin.amsterdam].
